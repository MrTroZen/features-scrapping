# Qavyo Restaurant Intelligence — Research Scope

## Purpose

This repository collects reusable restaurant operational knowledge that can help Qavyo understand:

- how restaurants operate
- how restaurant performance is measured
- how restaurant costs are calculated
- how operational problems are detected
- how restaurant managers make decisions
- which calculations and workflows can be automated
- which operational insights could eventually improve Qavyo

The repository is NOT intended to collect every piece of restaurant-related content.

Quality and operational usefulness are more important than volume.

# 4. CONTENT TO REJECT

Do not create structured knowledge from content that provides no meaningful restaurant operational information.

Examples:

- "work harder"
- "care about your customers"
- "post more on social media"
- "great restaurants build great brands"

unless the source provides specific operational methodology, data, calculations or evidence.

---

# 6. RESEARCH EXTRACTION STANDARD

When processing content, ask:

1. Does this teach something operationally useful?
2. Is there a measurable concept?
3. Is there a calculation?
4. Is there a decision rule?
5. Is there a restaurant problem?
6. Is there a useful management process?
7. Is there data Qavyo could realistically understand?
8. Could this help explain why performance changed?
9. Could this help a manager make a better decision?

If the answer to all of these is no, the content probably does not belong in the knowledge base.

---

# 7. DO NOT FORCE PRODUCT IDEAS

Research comes before product development.

It is acceptable for a useful restaurant insight to have:

Qavyo opportunity: None identified.

Do not invent product ideas simply to complete a template.

---

# 8. RESEARCH GOAL

The long-term goal is to create a restaurant operations knowledge base that can support questions such as:

- What should restaurant managers monitor every day?
- What should they review weekly?
- Which metrics indicate operational problems?
- Which metrics can Qavyo calculate automatically?
- What causes food-cost variance?
- What causes labour inefficiency?
- How should restaurants evaluate menu profitability?
- What can be detected by combining POS and inventory?
- What can be detected by combining sales and labour?
- Which recurring decisions could software support?
- Which operational problems repeatedly appear across sources?
