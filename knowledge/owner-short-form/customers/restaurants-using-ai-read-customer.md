# Key Information

- Stalking customers with AI. How can I get to know my customers on a deeper level? What you do is you take your customer list, name, email, phone number, and you upload it to a software like People Data Labs, which is AI data enrichment. From there, the software does a deep dive into all of the online profiles of those customers, and it spits out who your customer is on average. It summarizes the commonalities. A lot of restaurants think that their customer can be everyone, but all of the data show that that is not the case. every restaurant you can name from Domino's, which is the most generic possible brand I can even think of, actually has an ideal customer that is very specific.
- They make all of their marketing decisions around that. So, when you're able to use an AI tool like this to analyze your customer base, even if you serve a wide group of people, it will show you what the most common type of customer you have is.
- You can factor that in with all of your marketing decisions moving forward.
