# Key Information

- The number one mistake restaurant owners make when calculating food cost?
- Food cost % = Usage ÷ Sales × 100.
- Here s where most owners get it wrong: usage isn t the same as purchases.

# Numbers / Calculations

- For example, if you start with $2,000 in inventory, purchase $3,000, and end with $1,500, your actual usage is $3,500.
- If you did $10,000 in sales, your food cost is 35%.
