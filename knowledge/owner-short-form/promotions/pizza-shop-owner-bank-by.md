# Key Information

- The owner of Punksy Pizza invented this brilliant strategy that drives him over 20 new customers every month using direct mail. What Scott specifically does is he partners with a direct mail company called Moving Targets. They pull a list every month of everybody that has moved in to the zip codes around his pizzeria. He sends all of those people moving into his community this personalized letter. He welcomes them to the community.
- Gives them one free medium pizza with unlimited toppings. He sends less than 50 of these letters per month.
- They cost him under $2 each. His redemption rate is over 80%. There's typically 30 new movers into his town each month. So that means that he usually gets around 24 of them redeeming that offer like clockwork. Now, he does have a high food cost to factor in here as well. So let's say it has an average 30% food cost. That's now $13.33 to acquire customers that are worth thousands.
