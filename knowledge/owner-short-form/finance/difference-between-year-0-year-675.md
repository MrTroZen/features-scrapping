# Key Information

- Restaurant advice that sounds smart but can quietly hurt your business.
- Happy hour and BOGO deals create loyal regulars. Discounts often attract **deal-seekers**, not necessarily long-term loyal customers.
- Build loyalty through product, experience, and consistency.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- Train a reliable hourly employee to receive and verify deliveries while managers focus on sales, coaching, and guests.
