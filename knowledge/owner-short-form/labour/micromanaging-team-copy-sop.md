# Key Information

- If you have to stand behind your employees to get them to do it right, you don't have control.
- You have dependence.
- Micromanagement feels like accountability, but it creates a team that waits for the owner to catch every mistake.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- Your job is to build a team and system that catches it without you.
