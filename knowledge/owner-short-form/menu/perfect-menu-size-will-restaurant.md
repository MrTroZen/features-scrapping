# Key Information

- What's the ideal number of menu items for a restaurant? We've done a ton of research on this. The ideal number for maximizing profitability is six appetizers, which allows for just enough variety to cater to different tastes without overwhelming customers. Six also ensures that your kitchen can handle preparation efficiently. Beverages, we found the ideal number for is eight. Eight keeps the menu manageable.
- They tend to be significantly easier to make than food items. For entre, the ideal number is going to be eight.
- A focused selection of entre allows for better quality control, easier decision-making, and more efficient kitchen operations. Desserts are the final category, and the ideal number there is four. Fewer people order dessert in the first place.

# Useful Method / Takeaway
