# Key Information

- There are three steps separating a profitable menu versus one that loses money.
- Step number one, calculate the contribution margin of every single item on your menu.
- Step number two is to plot every item on your menu matrix using two axles, popularity versus contribution margin.

# Numbers / Calculations

- $18.50 outsells $19 every time.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- Place high margin items in the golden triangle where eyes naturally go, and never use round numbers.
