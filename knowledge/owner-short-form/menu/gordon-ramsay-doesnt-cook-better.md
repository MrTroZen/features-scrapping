# Key Information

- Gordon Ramsay has a golden rule in his restaurants, the 10 entree rule. The 10 entree rule means that he does not let his restaurants have more than 10 entrees.
- He knows that it increases decision fatigue.
- Makes it less likely that guests end up ordering at all if there's overwhelming choices. In addition to simplifying the guest experience, the other massive benefit it has is it drops food costs.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- Overall profitability through mastering the execution on those dishes.
