# Key Information

- Restaurant owners: average check and average cover measure two very different things.
- Imagine a family of 4 spends $100 at your restaurant.
- Average check: $100 per transaction.

# Numbers / Calculations

- Imagine a family of 4 spends $100 at your restaurant.
- Average check: $100 per transaction.
- Average cover: $100 ÷ 4 guests = $25 per guest.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- Both are falling: Investigate changes in pricing, menu mix, party size and purchasing behaviour before assuming you simply need more customers.
