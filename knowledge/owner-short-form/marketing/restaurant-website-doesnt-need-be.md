# Key Information

- Restaurant Owner A builds for conversion: clear value proposition, real customer reviews, strong Google visibility, and an obvious path to order.
- Restaurant Owner B builds for aesthetics: giant hero photos, beautiful design, but no clear CTA, reviews, or easy way to place an order.
- The other makes more money.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- Put your menu and ordering CTA where people can find them, show social proof, make your location and cuisine obvious, optimize pages for local search, and remove unnecessary clicks between landing on your site and placing an order.
