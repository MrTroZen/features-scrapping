# Key Information

- Posting more on social media won't fix a restaurant that nobody can find on Google.
- Social media can build awareness, but when someone is actively searching for restaurants near me, best pizza near me, or a specific dish, showing up in local search can put you directly in front of a customer who is ready to order.
- Your website, Google Business Profile, reviews, menu content, speed, and local SEO all contribute to whether you get discovered, or whether a competitor does.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- Check whether your website has clear location and cuisine signals, make your menu crawlable instead of hiding it in a PDF, keep your Google Business Profile complete and accurate, build consistent local citations, improve page speed, and make online ordering easy to find.
