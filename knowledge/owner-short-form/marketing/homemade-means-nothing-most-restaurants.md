# Key Information

- Most restaurants, when they say the word homemade, they're all lying.
- I've been in the industry for over a decade, and here's the truth.
- The FDA and USDA do not regulate the word homemade.

# Numbers / Calculations

- To make a pot of homemade soup at the restaurant, we're talking about two hours of labor and $40 in ingredients, and it may yield 20 soup bowls.
- Means $3.80 cost per bowl.
- Now, for comparison, a commercial base may cost $8 for that same 20 bowls.
- We're talking about $0.40 per bowl in cost.
