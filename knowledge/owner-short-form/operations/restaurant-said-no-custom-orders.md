# Key Information

- Do you say yes to everything the customers say to keep them happy? Here's what this owner did, and I think it's pretty smart. Step one is that he trained the team to say, "I'm sorry, ma'am. We do not allow customization at this time, but I'm more than happy to help you find something close to what you're craving today." Step two is that he updated the printed menu.
- Added, "Please note, we're unable to accommodate off-menu requests to ensure fast service for all guests." Step number three is that he analyzed.
- Requests came up most, so he added it as an add-on.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- Trained his team  to ask the guest to add that sauce. These are the three steps this owner took to ensure his guests feels heard, team stays sane, and the speed of service is not compromised for all customers.
