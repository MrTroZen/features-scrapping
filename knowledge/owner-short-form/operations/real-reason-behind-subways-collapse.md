# Key Information

- Subway didn t fail because people stopped eating sandwiches.
- It failed because the business model stopped working for the people running the restaurants.
- For years, Subway grew rapidly through franchising.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- The strongest restaurant systems create alignment between the brand, the franchise owners, and the customer experience because when the operators lose, the entire brand eventually suffers.
