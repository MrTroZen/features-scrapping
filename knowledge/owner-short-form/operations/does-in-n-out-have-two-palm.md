# Key Information

- What's really wild about the In-N-Out story is there's palm tree reference that one of the founders, Harry, liked from a movie called The Mad Mad World in 1963 that basically had two crossed palm trees that marked where the treasure was. So, he initially brought that into his location.
- Realized that customers understood that reference. So, he began doing that in all of his locations as well as having ferns up in the dining room.
- A bunch of other things that remind people of California cool. And again was able to anticipate that if he could become associated with each of these things in his customers minds that they would view his restaurant as.
