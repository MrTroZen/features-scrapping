# Key Information

- If you've ever been to an In-N-Out, you'll also know that everything they do is fresh. They have no freezers and they have no microwaves. Yet, they're able to offer price points that are actually lower than McDonald's. So, how do they do it?
- Building their own supply chain and distribution network where they've been able to build entire meat processing facilities and their own distribution network so that they can consistently bring fresh food to each of their locations and master each of the input ingredients that go in to making those burgers daily. It's also how they make hand cut fries from whole potatoes that are peeled, cut, and cooked fresh in-house every single day.
- Given them this huge advantage of being able to offer lowerc cost food and being known for freshness and quality.
