# Key Information

- The most Savvy operators use distressed asset buys to open new locations without having to spend six figures in buildout so you might be wondering why would they sell you their lease their equipment which literally cost them hundreds of thousands of dollars to set up because they're often in debt over those assets it means that they get charged every month for the real estate even if the restaurant isn't operational so by selling their assets to a new restaurant owner they can get themselves out of the debt and in exchange give you their equipment and other key items to Kickstart your new restaurant concept cheaply.
