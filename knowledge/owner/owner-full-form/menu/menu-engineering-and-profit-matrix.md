# Key Information

- Authoritative operational guide to restaurant menu engineering—the systematic discipline of architecting menu layout, pricing, and item composition to maximize unit profitability:
  1. **Demand-Driven Menu-Market Fit:** Using Google Keyword Planner data to identify high-volume local dish searches that lack a dominant go-to restaurant in the trade area (e.g. identifying local cravings for specific regional dishes).
  2. **The 4-Quadrant Menu Engineering Matrix (Kasavana & Smith / BCG Matrix):**
     - **Stars (High Profitability + High Popularity):** Core profit drivers. Maintain exact recipe specs, feature prominent photography, and protect visibility.
     - **Plowhorses (Low Profitability + High Popularity):** High-volume favorites with weak margins. Increase price incrementally, reduce portion size slightly, or substitute lower-cost secondary ingredients without sacrificing signature flavor.
     - **Puzzles (High Profitability + Low Popularity):** High-margin dishes that customers overlook. Rework menu descriptions, move to high-visibility layout zones, or train servers to proactively recommend them.
     - **Dogs (Low Profitability + Low Popularity):** Dead weight. Drain kitchen prep time and increase walk-in spoilage. Systematically remove from the menu.
  3. **The Z-Pattern & Golden Triangle Eye-Tracking Layout:** Eye-tracking studies demonstrate diners scan menus in a predictable path (top-left to top-right, down the diagonal, to bottom-right). Position top-margin "Stars" in the top-right corner and center-top sweet spots.
  4. **Sensory Description & Pricing Psychology:** Sensory culinary descriptions (origin, preparation technique, mouthfeel) lift perceived value and sales volume by up to 27%. Omitting dollar signs ($) reduces the psychological friction of spending.
  5. **Continuous Quarterly Pruning:** Auditing sales mix quarterly to eliminate the bottom 10% of stagnant SKUs, streamlining kitchen line throughput and inventory holding costs.

# Numbers / Calculations

- Unit profit impact: Rigorous menu engineering accounts for differences of up to $200,000+ in annual net owner distributions between comparable-volume restaurants.
- Description sales lift: Cornell University consumer psychology research proves that vivid sensory menu descriptions increase dish sales by up to 27%.
- Layout focus: Placing a dish in the top-right "sweet spot" of a physical or digital menu lifts that item's ordering share by 15% to 20%.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- **The Quarterly Menu Matrix Audit:** Export sales volume and ingredient costs from POS/inventory software into a 2x2 grid (Popularity vs Contribution Margin). Identify your top 3 Plowhorses and implement a $0.50 to $1.00 price increase or portion calibration.
- **Sensory Vocabulary Upgrade:** Replace plain ingredient lists with sensory triggers: replace "fried chicken" with "crisp, double-battered buttermilk fried chicken drizzled with hot honey."
- **Prune the Dogs Without Fear:** Operators fear customer backlash when cutting dishes; in reality, eliminating bottom-quartile items reduces prep complexity, shortens ticket times, and lowers waste with virtually zero impact on total cover counts.
