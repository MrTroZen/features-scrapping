# Key Information

- Case study of Scott Anthony, operator of Punxsy Pizza (Punxsutawney, Pennsylvania), operating in an ultra-competitive small-town market (5,700 population with 13 competing pizza shops—4x above national per-capita pizzeria density).
- Deploys a hyper-targeted "New Mover" direct mail campaign to systematically capture prospective diners before local dining habits form.
- Partners with a direct-mail list provider (Moving Targets) to extract new residential utility/deed move-ins, applying three strict demographic filters:
  1. **Out-of-Town Relocations Only:** Excludes internal address changes to guarantee the recipient does not have an established local pizza preference.
  2. **Family Household Status:** Targets multi-person families to capture higher average check sizes and repeat weekly group dining.
  3. **Household Income Threshold:** Filters for household incomes > $50,000 to attract guests willing to pay for premium quality rather than chasing bottom-tier commodity franchise discounts.
- Direct-mail format: A personalized letter arriving within 3 weeks of moving, addressed by name, featuring the founder's welcome, local community awards, high-resolution food imagery, and a high-value introductory certificate (free large pizza).

# Numbers / Calculations

- Market competition: 13 pizza operators in a small town of 5,700 people (4x national competitive density).
- Monthly campaign cost: Under $200.00 per month.
- Acquisition volume: Generates 24 to 27 verified new customers per month.
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC): Less than $10.00 per new dining customer acquired.
- Lifetime yield: Over 500 verified new mover recipients converted into multi-year regulars, generating hundreds of thousands of dollars in cumulative lifetime sales on less than $10,000 total multi-year direct-mail spend.
- Operator overall sales growth: Over 600% sales expansion over his operating tenure.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- **Habit-Formation Window:** Intercept new community residents within their first 21 days (before they try competitors or default to national delivery chains).
- **The Free Core Item Customer Investment:** Rather than sending weak "10% off" discount mailers that get thrown in the trash, offer an authentic free high-margin signature item (e.g. 1 free large pizza) as a customer acquisition investment.
- **Filter Before Sending:** Never blast mass unsegmented "Every Door Direct Mail" (EDDM) to general postal routes; filter strictly for out-of-area new families with sufficient disposable income.
