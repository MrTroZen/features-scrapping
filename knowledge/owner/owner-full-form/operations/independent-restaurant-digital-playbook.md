# Key Information

- In-depth operational masterclass on how independent restaurants can systematically compete against national chains and reclaim margin from third-party delivery aggregators (DoorDash, Uber Eats).
- **The Intent Mismatch in Social vs Search:** Social media reaches passive audiences with low immediate conversion intent, whereas Google Local SEO captures active high-intent dining searches at zero advertising cost.
- **The Delivery App Toll Booth Problem:** Third-party aggregators extract 20% to 30% per order while withholding guest data (phone, email, ordering history), converting loyal regulars into marketplace assets.
- **The Branded Mobile App Frequency Multiplier:** When prompted during checkout, over 50% of repeat direct diners download the restaurant's branded mobile app. Having the restaurant app icon on the smartphone home screen eliminates ordering friction and doubles guest re-order frequency compared to mobile websites.
- **Precision Digital Retargeting:** Retargeting online basket abandoners with a small 5% discount or reminder ad generates consistent 10:1 ROAS due to minimal audience size and ultra-high purchase intent.
- **Founder Narrative as Brand Moat:** Independent restaurants cannot win on national TV ad budgets or multi-million-dollar tech R&D, but hold a decisive competitive moat in authentic founder heritage and grandmother recipes.

# Numbers / Calculations

- Third-party delivery commission drag: 20% to 30% extracted per order by delivery aggregators.
- Mobile App adoption: Over 50% of direct online ordering guests adopt a branded restaurant mobile app within 30 days when prompted at checkout.
- App ordering frequency: Guests ordering via a branded mobile app order at **2x the frequency** and generate higher average check sizes compared to web-only diners.
- Retargeting efficiency: Cart abandonment retargeting campaigns on Meta platforms achieve up to a 10:1 ROAS ($10 return per $1 spent).
- Pareto regular distribution: The top 20% of loyal regulars generate approximately 80% of net profits and long-term customer lifetime value.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- **Checkout App-Prompt Trigger:** At the online ordering confirmation screen, offer diners a prompt: "Order through our App next time for 1-click re-orders and exclusive VIP rewards."
- **Home-Screen Friction Reduction:** Eliminating the multi-step friction of searching Google, navigating mobile web pages, and re-entering credit cards is the single biggest driver of repeat frequency.
- **Lifecycle Story Delivery:** Drip the restaurant's founding story, chef background, and culinary values over the first 3 automated welcome emails to build emotional defense against commodity discounting.
