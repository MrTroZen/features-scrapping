# Key Information

- Masterclass on multi-unit operational discipline with Mike Bausch, founder of Andolini's Worldwide (scaled from a single pizzeria to 12 high-volume units in Tulsa, Oklahoma; author of *UNSLICED*):
- **The Weekly Email Revenue Engine:** Rejecting the passive "email once a month" operator myth. Sending a weekly food-focused email drives predictable, attributable sales. Infrequent emailing degrades deliverability, increases spam reports, and causes list churn.
- **Email as Visual Social Feed:** Overcoming email paralysis by treating email sends like Instagram posts: high-resolution hero food photography, concise personal storytelling, and a prominent ordering link rather than long newsletters.
- **Geographic Market Concentration (Density Over Dispersion):** Built 12 locations within a single metro market (Tulsa) rather than expanding prematurely into distant states. Regional density consolidates commissary prep, centralizes management oversight, lowers distributor delivery minimums, and builds dominant local SEO authority.
- **The "Glorified Employee" Growth Trap:** Scaling store count without disciplined unit economics and delegable management systems merely multiplies operational stress and overhead without increasing owner net distributions.
- **Rigorous Recipe Costing & True Prime Cost:** Mandating precise, weighed recipe cards for every menu item down to the ounce; identifying hidden labor costs (payroll taxes, salaried managers, owner sweat equity) often omitted from superficial labor calculations.

# Numbers / Calculations

- Email send revenue: A single weekly email send to an engaged direct customer database yields **$5,000 to $8,000 in tracked, direct revenue every single week** ($7,000 weekly average).
- Email open rates: Direct restaurant loyalty email lists average ~40% open rates (dwarfing organic social reach which delivers posts to <5% of followers).
- Scale footprint: Grew from 1 independent suburban pizzeria to 12 multi-concept locations across the Tulsa, Oklahoma metro area.
- Training ROI hurdle: Training and operational documentation investments should target a minimum **5x return** in reduced waste, lower turnover, and improved labor efficiency.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- **The Weekly Email Rhythm:** Send direct customer emails on a consistent weekly cadence (typically Tuesday/Wednesday ahead of weekend ordering peaks); maintain brevity with 1 striking dish photo, 2 sentences of context, and a direct order button.
- **Weighed Recipe Card Enforcement:** Eliminate "free-pouring" and line guesswork by weighing cheese, meats, and toppings in grams/ounces; display laminated recipe spec sheets at every prep station.
- **Cluster Expansion Strategy:** Expand to 5 to 10 units within your initial 30-mile home radius before contemplating out-of-market geographic expansion to leverage existing brand recognition and operational management bandwidth.
