# Key Information

- Operational case study of Phnom Penh Noodle Shack (Long Beach, California), an established, multi-generational family Cambodian restaurant modernizing its digital operations.
- Operator transitioned management focus from working in the kitchen to working on business systems and digital guest convenience.
- **The Social Media vs Direct Ordering Gap:** Discontinued low-return daily organic social posting in favor of local Google search capture paired with a frictionless direct mobile ordering flow.
- **Delivery Marketplace Commission Relief:** Transitioned regular diners away from high-commission delivery apps (DoorDash, Uber Eats) to a dedicated, branded restaurant mobile app.
- **Mobile App Conversion Funnel:** Implemented a non-intrusive mobile browser banner ("Get the best experience on our mobile app: save time, earn rewards, get exclusive deals") that converts one-off website browsers into permanent mobile app users.

# Numbers / Calculations

- Direct digital sales growth: Increased online ordering sales by 200% within a 6-month period following direct digital system deployment.
- Margin preservation: Eliminated standard 20% to 30% third-party marketplace commissions on converted repeat diners.
- Rating footprint: Secured 5-star mobile app ratings on the Apple App Store, improving brand trust and search visibility.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- **Mobile Web-to-App Conversion Banner:** Place a sticky banner across the bottom of the mobile web ordering page highlighting tangible user benefits (saved payment methods, 1-tap re-ordering, loyalty rewards) to prompt app download on the first visit.
- **Menu Categorization for Speed:** Structure the mobile app menu around historical re-order staples (signature noodle bowls, broths, porridge) with pre-configured size and modifier defaults to achieve sub-60-second checkout.
- **Automated Rewards Enrollment:** Automatically enroll guest phone numbers at mobile checkout into the rewards database rather than requiring separate physical punch cards or complicated registration portals.
