# Key Information

- Let's price a real catering order for 85 guests.
- Price equals total cost divided by 1 minus your profit margin.
- In this particular case, we know that the total cost is $713.

# Numbers / Calculations

- In this particular case, we know that the total cost is $713.
- $360 for food, $250 for labor, $50 for supplies, and $50 delivery.
- Comes out to $838.
- Now, per person, if we divide the $838 by 85 guests, that comes out to around $9.87 per person, plus tax.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- Now, the total margin we would like out of this catering deal, imagine is 15%.
