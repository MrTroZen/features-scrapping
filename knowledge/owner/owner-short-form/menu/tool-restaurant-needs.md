# Key Information

- Reason number one restaurants fail is not having menu market fit. How much does your menu fit your local market's needs? You can check exactly how many people want your type of food or your menu items in your area for free online. You go into Google's keyword planner tool, which they make public.
- Free online, and you type in some terms that are related to your concept. Like if I were starting an Italian restaurant, say in Santa Monica, I would type in terms like Italian restaurant, pasta, pizza, and then select Santa Monica as the location in Google.
- I could get the volumes of people that are searching for terms that are related to my restaurant or menu in that area every month to help me understand how many people are actively searching for what I plan on offering.
