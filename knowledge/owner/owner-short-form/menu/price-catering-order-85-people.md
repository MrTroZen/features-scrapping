# Key Information

- Let's price a pizza catering order for 85 guests together.
- Price equals total cost divided by 1 minus your profit margin.
- Let's start with the total cost.

# Numbers / Calculations

- Food purchases for this order is $400, labor is $300, supplies and disposables around 50, and delivery is another $50.
- Now, that puts our total cost at $800.
- Now, 800 divided by 1 minus 45% gives you $1,454 for the full order.
- So, now 1454 divided by 85, and that gets you $17 per person.
