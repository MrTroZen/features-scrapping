# Key Information

- The best restaurant leaders never ask their team to do something they wouldn t do themselves.
- When Syed Pasha, owner of @portofperiperi, opens a new location, he doesn t sit in the office.
- He gets on the line, works the grill, helps the team, and shows them exactly what his standards look like.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- Lead from the front, set the standard, then build systems that make that standard repeatable.
