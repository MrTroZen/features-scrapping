# Key Information

- I used to crank out hundreds of pizzas every single day, working side-by-side with our crew, and I thought I was killing it.
- I realized working a $12 an hour job doesn't actually help my team.
- There is a concept in economics called the opportunity cost, where every hour you spend doing one thing is an hour you can't spend on something more valuable.

# Numbers / Calculations

- I realized working a $12 an hour job doesn't actually help my team.
