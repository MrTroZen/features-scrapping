# Key Information

- I used to think that more options on a menu would mean more sales because it means that guests have even more to choose from that could match their tastes.
- All of the data shows that the exact opposite is true because when guests see too many choices, they freeze up.
- It's called decision fatigue in psychology.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- So, you're not just making it easier for guests to order, you're also making it easier for your team to  execute.
