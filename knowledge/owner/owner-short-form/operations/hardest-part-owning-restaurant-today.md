# Key Information

- Your best manager still can't fire anyone without you in the room and that's not a trust problem, it's a training gap.
- The restaurants scaling past the owner's personal bandwidth all have one thing in common: a system for teaching judgment, not just tasks.
- Start today: have your next new manager sit through 5 real interviews with you before they touch one solo.

# Numbers / Calculations

- After each one, ask them one question "would you hire this person, and why?" Then build a hard checkpoint at 30 and 60 days (a menu quiz, a time trial, anything measurable) so "are they working out" has a scheduled answer instead of dragging on for months.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- Do this and firing stops feeling like a confrontation it's just following the process everyone agreed to on day one.
