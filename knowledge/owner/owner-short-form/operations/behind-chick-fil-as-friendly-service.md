# Key Information

- Here's how Ritz Carlton  helped Chick-fil-A turn things around. Back in 2001, Chick-fil-A had just crossed 10,000 stores, but their service was mid. True. Cathy, the founder, visited Ritz Carlton in NYC.
- Noticed something that every time he said, "Thank you." The staff replied  with, "My pleasure." Then in 2003, his son Dan made it non-negotiable.
- It became the signature of Chick-fil-A service. Dan also brought in the COO of Ritz Carlton.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- The fine dining legend Danny Myers, who later founded the Shake Shack. They created what's called the core four.  Create eye contact, share a smile, speak with enthusiastic tone, and make it personal. And the core four became the foundation of Chick-fil-A's hospitality.
