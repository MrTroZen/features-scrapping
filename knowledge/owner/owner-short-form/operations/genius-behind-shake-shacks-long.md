# Key Information

- When Danny started Shake Shack, he chose a strategically small location, just 900 square feet, even though he had millions of dollars.
- This small footprint meant the restaurant always looked busy with a line out the door, creating instant social proof for people passing by.
- Danny didn't stop there.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- What's so counterintuitive about this is that a lot of restaurant owners think to themselves, "My restaurant, if it's in a larger location, will look even more impressive to guests." But in reality, the opposite tends to be more true.
