# Key Information

- Three numbers that actually tell you if your restaurant is profitable.
- First, sales per labor hour.
- Divide your total daily sales by total labor hours worked.

# Numbers / Calculations

- For casual concepts, you want that number to be between $25 to $35 or higher.
- If your goal is $28 average check and your actual is $25, you just lost 10% of your potential revenue without a single customer complaint or a bad review.
- Most profitable restaurants stay under 60%.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- Keeps your average check on target without relying on your team to remember to upsell every order.
