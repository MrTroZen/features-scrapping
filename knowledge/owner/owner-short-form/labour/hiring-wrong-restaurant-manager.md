# Key Information

- If your restaurant manager doesn't have this one quality, it's a sign they're the wrong fit for your restaurant. It first comes down to ensuring your manager has an owner mindset. When I'm interviewing candidates for this trait, my favorite question is what they did in some of their past roles.
- Asking them what their favorite parts of the role or their frustrations with the company were. Because then you ask, if you were the owner, what would you have done differently to improve it? Because if they operate with an owner's mindset in every role they take, they've probably thought deeply about how to improve the business beyond just their specific role.
