# Key Information

- Are you running a discount promo?

# Numbers / Calculations

- Say your burger meal is $20.
- Your prime cost is $12 and your contribution margin for this dish is $8.
- So, instead of keeping that contribution margin of $8, you're keeping $6 per order.
- Now, you run a 10% discount on this meal and the meal drops to $18.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- Now, after the discount, you need to sell 133 burgers, the same burger meals, to make that same contribution margin of $800.
