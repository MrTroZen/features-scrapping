# Key Information

- Chick-fil-A doubled their sales by closing on Sundays, the second most popular day for fast food restaurants. Doesn't that cost them 15% or more of their sales? Chick-fil-A's founder was a devout Christian, like 67% of Americans are. And he realized that if he could demonstrate through his actions that he cared deeply about observing Sundays.
- Wanted his team to do the same, the loyalty that he'd get from the Christian community would more than compensate for any loss in sales from that day. They're saying through their actions that they value God over any profit incentives. The results prove that that decision was one of the wisest he could have made. They actually make more in sales than Starbucks, Subway, and McDonald's combined per.
