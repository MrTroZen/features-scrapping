# Key Information

- Let's calculate Rev Pash together.
- Revenue per available seat hour.
- Total sales divided by number of seats times hours open.

# Numbers / Calculations

- Your restaurant makes $4,000 during a 5h hour dinner service.
- Now that means each seat is generating $10 per hour.
- Now, your ref pash for casual dining should sit somewhere around $8 to $12.
- For fast casuals, between $12 to $18.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- If your ref pash is low, you can adjust pricing, improve table turnover, or fill slower day parts, adding 100% commissionfree catering through owner.com.
