# Key Information

- Use AI to find what customers already want Export 3 12 months of line-item sales from your POS, including order ID, item, modifiers, time and channel.
- Clean it: merge duplicate item names and remove voids and comps.
- Upload the file to Claude or ChatGPT and ask: "Group by order ID, run a market basket analysis, and give me the top 20 item pairs by lift, with support, confidence and add-on margin." Shortlist pairs where lift is above 2 but the attach rate is low.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- Put 2 3 popular, high-margin items on a house list as "best sellers," plus 1 2 high-margin slow movers as "chef's picks." When asked what's good, ask one question ("Hearty or lighter?"), then offer two options: a best seller first, a chef's pick second, each with a sensory reason.
