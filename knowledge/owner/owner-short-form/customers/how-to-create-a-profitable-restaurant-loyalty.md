# Key Information

- You could still lose money with a loyalty program if you're giving people points unless you do this next key you need to choose the right items to reward on your loyalty program this is a powerful economic Hack That restaurants have used forever to make their guests feel like they're getting amazing value but without killing profits it's all about perceived value versus actual cost you want customers to feel like they're receiving a high value item when in reality that item doesn't cost you very much money at all and I'm sure you know those items on your menu that customers love but fortunately they aren't that expensive to make.
