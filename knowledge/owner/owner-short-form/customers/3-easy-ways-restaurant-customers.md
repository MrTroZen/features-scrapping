# Key Information

- Here's three steps to stop leaking loyalty. First, you build your loyalty program on giving away lots of lowcost.
- High perceived value rewards from your menu. Think appetizers, beverages, and desserts that will cost you almost nothing,.
- Customers feel like they're getting great value. The second step is you automate your loyalty reminders. You set up messages that automatically remind customers about their loyalty points when they haven't been back in a while. And then the third step, you gify the experience. That means making rewards feel like a game.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- It gives them another reason to want to order again.
