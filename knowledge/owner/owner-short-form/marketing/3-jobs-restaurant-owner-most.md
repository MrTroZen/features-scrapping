# Key Information

- If you're still on the line every night, you're not the hardest worker in your restaurant.
- You're the most expensive problem in it.
- Most restaurant owners fall into what operators call the operator trap.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- The third job, the one that actually scales your restaurant and builds real wealth, gets whatever energy is left at the end of a 14-hour day.
