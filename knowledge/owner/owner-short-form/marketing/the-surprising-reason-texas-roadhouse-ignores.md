# Key Information

- Texas Roadhouse is a multi-billion dollar brand yet crazy enough they spend almost all of their advertising dollars on local not National advertising unlike the big Brands like Applebees and Olive Garden that focus on these National marketing strategies like television ads and radio ads and Facebook ads the vast majority of Texas Roadhouse's focus is on making unique local events so much so that they've added a role at each of their locations called local events marketer whose entire job is to reach out to all of the organizations in the community and create Partnerships and events that appeal to the different things that are happening there this makes that brand feel more connected to the people that live in those towns and more authentic than national chain that doesn't necessarily care about the community because they've been able to achieve that connection in each of the towns they're in they are getting the benefit of operating like a local restaurant where they can have deep relationships in the community even though they're the multi-billion Dollar business that they are.
