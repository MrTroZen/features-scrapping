# Key Information

- There's three steps to using menu engineering. Step one is identifying what those most profitable.
- Popular items on your menu are. They tend to be appetizers, beverages, and desserts that have the lowest food cost. Step two is building the human systems around pushing those items. That means things like having your servers trained.
- Incentivized on upselling appetizers, beverages, and desserts. Step three, using systems, technology, automation, and menu design to push those low food cost to high margin items. Have your menu specifically designed to promote your appetizers or have your online ordering system.
