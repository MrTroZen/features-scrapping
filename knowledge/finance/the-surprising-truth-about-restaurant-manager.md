# Key Information

- In order to have a really big business you've got to incentivize people with your success and help them understand how they can grow with you because what that solves for as a restaurant owner is having those managers that we've all dealt with that just call you whenever something goes wrong and asks you what to do and can't operate independently instead of being in the trenches proactively and not only solving problems as they come up but preventing problems through helping you improve the business versus pulling you in to every little detail that's what's required incentivizing them with your long-term success and making them feel included in that success you don't have to make explicit promises but I would say I want to set up a managing partner model where our top managers become managing partners of each location and end up being able to share in the profitability of those locations so that as we build something valuable your compensation increases with time and your ability to scale yourself as an owner grows over time with the success of this concept.
