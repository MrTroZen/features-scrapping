# Key Information

- We spend around 40 to 45,000 a month on miscellaneous.
- All the packaging and disposables including our pizza boxes, $10,000 a month.
- $1,000 a month in lease equipment like the dishwasher and the ice machine.

# Numbers / Calculations

- All the packaging and disposables including our pizza boxes, $10,000 a month.
- $1,000 a month in lease equipment like the dishwasher and the ice machine.
- Credit card processing fees, $5,000 a month.
- Replenishing broken stuff, music licensing, waste removal, plus admin and office supplies, $2,000 a month.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- Despite all the expenses, this store runs very profitably at 10 to 15% profit margin after a decade of being open.
