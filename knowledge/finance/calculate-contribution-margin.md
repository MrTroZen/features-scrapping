# Key Information

- How to calculate contribution margin for every single dish and why it matters.
- Contribution margin equals menu price minus food cost.
- Let's compare two items both priced at $10.

# Numbers / Calculations

- Let's compare two items both priced at $10.
- Pizza food cost is $3, so the contribution margins is $7.
- If, for instance, your fixed cost for the week is $10,000, at $7 contribution margin for your pizzas, you're going to need $1,428 pizzas to be sold to cover that fixed cost.
- Wings food cost is $6, so the contribution margin is $4.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- This is why pushing low profit margin items is going to make you busy but not profitable.
