# Key Information

- Cracker Barrel just gave restaurant owners a masterclass in how to destroy brand loyalty.
- They tried to attract younger customers by removing the antiques and replacing their iconic 47-year-old logo.
- They changed the exact experience their core customers were coming back for: familiarity, nostalgia, and consistency.

# Numbers / Calculations

- Traffic reportedly dropped more than 10% before the company reversed course.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- Listen to the data: Track traffic, repeat visits, reviews, and sales before and after major changes.
