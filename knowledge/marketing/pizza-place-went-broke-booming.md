# Key Information

- Metro Pizza was losing $10,000+ every month and the problem wasn t their pizza.
- Their old website was slow, their online ordering experience was clunky, and too many customers were being pushed toward expensive third-party platforms.
- They rebuilt their website with better visuals and a simpler ordering flow, launched a branded app with one-tap ordering and Apple Pay, and added a hybrid delivery system that prioritized their own drivers.

# Numbers / Calculations

- Metro Pizza was losing $10,000+ every month and the problem wasn t their pizza.
- $112,000+ in direct online sales per month, up more than 54%.
- Their average ticket grew from $60 to $68, and their new app generated 11,000+ downloads in its first 90 days.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- The lesson: sometimes the fastest way to grow restaurant sales isn t getting more customers it s making it easier for the customers you already have to order directly from you.
