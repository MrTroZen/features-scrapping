# Key Information

- Most operators focus on the entree, the plating, the service.
- Your guests are already forming an opinion the moment something hits the table.
- The bread course is one of the most underutilized tools in a restaurant.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- Look at your table experience tonight and ask yourself what the first impression is actually saying about your restaurant.
