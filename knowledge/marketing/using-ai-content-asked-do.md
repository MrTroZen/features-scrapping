# Key Information

- If your customers can tell you re using AI, you re probably using it wrong.
- Let AI write your recipes Let your chef create the food.
- Use AI to argue with negative reviews Use it to understand what your customers are saying.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- Instead of using AI to generate content nobody asked for, use it to turn your restaurant's data into actions that increase revenue, protect your margins and keep customers coming back.
