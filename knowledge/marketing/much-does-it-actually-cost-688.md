# Key Information

- Most people see a busy restaurant and think, They must be making a fortune.
- The reality is very different.
- Before a restaurant ever sees profit, there are serious monthly costs: Food and ingredients Payroll and benefits Rent Utilities Insurance Repairs, supplies, packaging, fees, and all the little things that add up fast.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- Loyalty, repeat business, and word of mouth compound over time.
