# Key Information

- Your restaurant website might be losing orders before guests even see your menu.
- Make it flashy. Prioritize mobile speed, readable text, real food photos, and one obvious Order Online button.
- Just upload the PDF menu. Use an interactive menu with categories, modifiers, dietary details, prices, and checkout built.
