# Key Information

- Catering is really a logistics operation!
- Every operator thinks a big order just means bigger margins.
- Nobody accounts for what's actually happening behind the scenes: when things hit the oven, when they come out for packaging, how long setup takes, who's serving on-site, and getting every tray back to the store washed and accounted.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- The operators who actually profit from catering aren't just cooking more, they've mapped labor down to the hour.
