# Key Information

- When Ron was starting what would become Panera Bread, he noticed his customer base starting to act very strange in sneaking in deli meat.
- Starting to pull apart his bread in front of him to make sandwiches. They would even sneak in their own coffee.
- They loved his bread quality.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- Cafe to take advantage of that customer rule-breaking behavior. That's when things absolutely took off for him. His first unit went from struggling cookie store to quickly reaching multiple millions of dollars.
