# Key Information

- The key to franchising success is having a huge amount of franchisee demand ready to go. When Mo.
- Omar made the decision to franchise Talkin Tacos, they were getting multiple emails every month of people who noticed the lines out the door of their Takaria. Having that demand from the start is really important.
- Small franchises with less than 10 locations are a death trap. But I've seen bankrupt restaurant owners promising concepts. Here's why. Restaurant franchises are extremely expensive to start.

# Numbers / Calculations

- Maintain. Recent research by Fran Data proves that it costs franchisers an average of $70,000 per unit to support franchises with training systems, legal, and quality assurance.
