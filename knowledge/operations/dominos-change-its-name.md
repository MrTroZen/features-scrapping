# Key Information

- Domino's wasn't initially called Domino's. It was called Domino's, which was the name of the original concept that the old owner of the pizzeria that got converted into Domino's called it. It was a family focused pizzeria. Tom, the then founder of Domino's, decided to rename it to Domino's.
- He knew that he needed something that appealed more to his ideal customer, which he envisioned as being young men rather than families. And during this period of the early 1960s, Domino's was one of the most popular gambling games for young men. So, he decided that this was the perfect name for appealing to them. He wanted his customers to associate fun.
- Playing games with friends with his pizza.
