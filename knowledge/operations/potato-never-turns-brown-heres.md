# Key Information

- Most restaurants lose time every single day to prep steps that nobody questions.
- Soaking potatoes to prevent browning is one of them.
- Bioengineered potatoes that do not oxidize are now available, and the operators using them are cutting prep time, reducing waste, and removing one more thing that can go wrong in a busy kitchen.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- Look at your prep process and ask yourself how many steps exist just to work around a problem that has already been solved.
