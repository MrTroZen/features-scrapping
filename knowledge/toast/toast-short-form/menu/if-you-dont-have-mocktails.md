# Key Information

- While production costs are lower without alcohol, guests still want to enjoy a highquality drink with ingredients worthy of their dollar.
- Be sure to price your mocktails appropriately.
- Train your staff to recommend these options, especially to designated drivers or guests who mention they're not drinking.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- Integrate standardized operational workflows for if you dont have mocktails into daily pre-shift briefings and manager shift checklists.
