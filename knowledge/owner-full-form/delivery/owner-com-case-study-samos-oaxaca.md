# Key Information

- Case study of SaMo's Oaxaca, an independent family-owned Mexican restaurant in Santa Monica, California, struggling with pandemic dining restrictions and aggressive third-party delivery marketplace fees.
- Relying exclusively on third-party aggregators depleted restaurant margins through steep commission deductions while concealing direct customer relationships and ordering data.
- Transitioning guest ordering from aggregator marketplaces to a branded direct digital ordering system eliminated commission leakage and restored direct communications with guests.
- Owning customer contact information (email addresses and order histories) enabled automated email campaigns (e.g. restaurant anniversary celebrations and event invitations) that drive repeat visit frequency.

# Numbers / Calculations

- Third-party delivery marketplaces charged approximately 30% commission per order on delivery sales.
- Generated approximately $180,000 in direct online orders in a single year through the direct ordering channel.
- Avoided tens of thousands of dollars in annual marketplace commission fees (at 30% take-rate, ~$180,000 in direct volume represents ~$54,000 in potential commission savings).

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- **Direct Order Migration:** Provide an easy, frictionless direct online ordering experience to shift regulars away from 30% commission third-party platforms.
- **First-Party Customer Database:** Collect guest email addresses and order history directly to power regular re-engagement emails and milestone promotions without paying advertising fees.

# Context

- Operator: Juliana and her husband, SaMo's Oaxaca (Santa Monica, California).
- Platform: Owner.com.
- Environment: Independent small family-owned restaurant maintaining solvency and profitability post-pandemic by reclaiming customer ownership from delivery apps.
