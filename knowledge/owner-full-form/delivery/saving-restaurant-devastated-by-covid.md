# Key Information

- Case study of The Original Pizza Cookery (Granada Hills, Los Angeles), an established independent restaurant severely impacted by COVID-19 lockdown restrictions and dine-in closures.
- Overhauls takeout and direct ordering to replicate consumer e-commerce experiences (one-click re-ordering, high-resolution food photography, real-time SMS status tracking).
- Replaces reliance on high-commission third-party delivery marketplaces (DoorDash, Uber Eats) by converting customers to a direct branded digital ordering channel.
- Captures first-party guest data (names, phone numbers, order histories) at checkout to enable automated retention campaigns.

# Numbers / Calculations

- Over $4,500 in additional direct online ordering sales generated within the first few days of implementation.
- Core pizza food cost estimated at ~30%, while automated add-ons (mozzarella sticks, side salads, desserts) operate at significantly lower food cost (~5%), boosting overall ticket margins.
- Traditional third-party delivery commissions avoided (typically 20% to 30% per order).

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- **Visual High-Margin Upselling:** Pair ~30% food-cost core entrees with low-food-cost (~5%) add-on recommendations in the digital checkout flow to expand ticket margin.
- **First-Party Data Ownership:** Capture guest contact info directly during checkout rather than ceding data to marketplace platforms, feeding automated re-order reminders.

# Context

- Operator: Jordan Klempner, The Original Pizza Cookery (Los Angeles, CA).
- Platform: Owner.com (formerly Profitboss).
- Environment: Post-lockdown independent pizza restaurant recovering lost dine-in traffic via digital delivery and takeout transformation.
