# Multi-Unit Operational Excellence: Distressed Asset Turnarounds, Purchasing Budgets, and Offshore Back-Office Systems

## Overview
A comprehensive operational teardown of Matanga's Pizzeria (San Antonio, TX), founded by former engineers Anga and Matt. The study documents how the operators turned an insolvent $500k acquisition losing -$200k/year into a 7-unit, **$7,000,000+ system generating 8% net profit ($560k+ cash flow)** while scaling without outside debt or equity. Key systems include video-based training with objective time trials, rent-to-sales capacity formulas, purchasing-based COGS control, and offshore back-office management.

---

## 1. Distressed Asset Purchase Valuation & Capacity Modeling
When evaluating vacant or failing restaurant spaces (e.g., failed franchise units), operators evaluate the hard physical assets (hoods, walk-ins, commercial ovens, HVAC) rather than past revenue or brand goodwill.

### The 8% Rent Capacity Formula:
To achieve a minimum 10% unit-level net profit margin, gross base rent plus CAM must not exceed **8.0% of gross sales**.

$$\text{Required Minimum Gross Sales} = \frac{\text{Monthly Rent Obligation}}{0.08}$$

$$\text{Weekly Sales Threshold} = \frac{\text{Required Minimum Monthly Sales}}{4.33}$$

$$\text{Required Weekly Guest Count} = \frac{\text{Weekly Sales Threshold}}{\text{Average Check Size}}$$

### Worked Example:
- **Monthly Lease Cost:** $10,000/month.
- **Required Monthly Sales:** $\frac{\$10,000}{0.08} = \$125,000/\text{month}$.
- **Required Weekly Sales:** $\approx \$28,870 - \$30,000/\text{week}$.
- **Order Volume at $30 Average Ticket:**
  $$\frac{\$30,000}{\$30} = 1,000 \text{ orders / week} \quad (\approx 143 \text{ orders / day})$$

> **Decision Rule:** If trade-area demographics, vehicular traffic counts, and delivery density within a 4-mile radius cannot support 1,000 transactions per week, the lease rate is mathematically insolvent regardless of equipment discounts.

## 2. Onboarding Automation & Objective Time Trials
To eliminate a 15-hour weekly owner drain repeating basic training for high-turnover entry-level staff:
- **Modular iPhone Training Modules:** Short video modules filmed at 5:00 AM covering line assembly, dough stretching, and closing procedures.
- **Verification Gating:** New hires must complete video modules and pass a digital menu/operations quiz before being scheduled for paid floor shifts.
- **Objective Time Trial Standards:** Replaced subjective supervisor feedback ("you are working too slow") with quantified operational targets:
  - **Closing Makeline Teardown Standard:**
    - $\le 15 \text{ minutes} \implies$ **Meets Standard / Exceeds Expectation**.
    - $> 20 \text{ minutes} \implies$ **Fails Operational Standard**.
  - **Kitchen Load Time:** Standardized tracking from order firing to makeline tablet bump into the oven deck.

## 3. Strict Operating Targets: Labor & Purchasing Budgets

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│             DAILY UNIT-LEVEL KPI CONTROL               │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • Labor Target: 19.0% of Sales (POS Real-Time)         │
│ • Daily Public Reporting to Team Communication Channel │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • Food Cost Control: Weekly Purchasing Budget (29-30%)  │
│   e.g., $10,000 Allocated Weekly Food Truck Budget     │
│   Truck 1: $5,500 used ──► Remaining Balance: $4,500   │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Target Labor Percentage:** Standardized at **19.0% of gross sales** (POS real-time wage calculation before employer taxes). Reported daily in manager group chats.
- **Purchasing-Based COGS Control:** Rather than relying solely on post-hoc theoretical inventory variance calculations, management enforces a **pre-order weekly purchasing budget pegged to 29.0%–30.0% of projected sales**. If sales drop mid-week, the remaining purchase order allowance is adjusted downward before supplier trucks dispatch.

## 4. Local Guerrilla Referral Seeding
- **Hotel Concierge B2B Seeding:**
  - Identifies 100 hotels and local businesses within a 4-mile delivery radius.
  - Divides into 4 cohorts of 25 businesses visited on a strict 4-week rotating schedule.
  - Frontline managers deliver fresh 10-inch pizzas or garlic knots directly to front-desk staff.
  - **Scripting:** *"We receive significant guest volume from this hotel. We wanted to thank you for recommending us with complimentary food."* This creates reciprocity and drives front-desk staff to steer tourists to the pizzeria.
- **Rush-Hour Mascot Sign-Spinning:** Scheduling a frontline team member in a branded costume on adjacent traffic corridors during peak commuter hours (5:00–6:00 PM). At $15/hr labor cost, the visual repetition captures hundreds of daily commuter impressions.

## 5. Habit-Forming Lifecycle Messaging
- **Schedule Predictability:** Dispatches two emails and two SMS blasts weekly on fixed days and hours to build subconscious buying habits:
  - **Wednesday at 4:00 PM:** "Hump Day" value promotion paired with community/team storytelling.
  - **Friday at 4:00 PM:** "Feed the Family Friday" bundle promotion.
- **Performance Benchmarks:**
  - Maintains **35% to 38% email open rates**.
  - Directly drives **40% of total recurring customer volume** (~$2.8M to $3.0M in attributable annual sales).

---

## 6. Offshore Back-Office Infrastructure (Virtual Assistants)
- **Staffing Model:** Employs 5–6 full-time virtual assistants in the Philippines ($4.00–$5.00/hour via OnlineJobs.ph) to handle all non-guest-facing administrative tasks.
- **Centralized Dashboard Management:** Consolidates 30+ disparate data points across POS (Toast), direct online ordering (Owner), and scheduling software into a unified multi-unit tracking spreadsheet.
- **AI-Augmented Review Moderation:** VAs triage 100% of incoming customer reviews using custom AI prompts instructed to:
  - Lead with genuine small-business owner gratitude.
  - Redirect negative complaints offline to private email/phone channels to resolve before public escalation.
- **Batching Principle:** Promotion calendars, social media copy, and digital email blasts are drafted by VAs on the 25th of each month for founder sign-off, eliminating daily ad-hoc decision fatigue.
