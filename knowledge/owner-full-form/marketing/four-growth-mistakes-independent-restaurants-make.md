# Key Information

- Breaks down four common digital marketing mistakes that drain independent restaurant budgets without generating measurable customer orders:
  1. **Boosting Social Media Posts:** Clicking the in-app "Boost Post" button on Facebook/Instagram delivers ads to passive users browsing for entertainment rather than active dining discovery. Produces vanity "likes" without orders.
  2. **Unmeasured Food Influencer Partnerships:** Paying cash or comping expensive meals to regional food influencers without unique promo codes, localized audience auditing, or direct tracking. Influencer follower bases often reside outside the restaurant's delivery/dine-in trade radius.
  3. **Broad/Sloppy Ad Targeting:** Running ad campaigns targeted broadly at entire metropolitan areas without utilizing custom audiences (past phone/email lists) or 14-day website retargeting.
  4. **The "Leaky Bucket" Conversion Problem:** Spending time and money to drive traffic to an unoptimized digital storefront (e.g. unreadable PDF menus, slow-loading mobile sites, or third-party delivery links that extract 30% fees and leak customers to competitors).

# Numbers / Calculations

- Boosted post ROI: Paying $15 to $50 on boosted posts delivers near-zero attributable customer acquisition compared to Google Local SEO or retargeting.
- Third-party commission leakage: Funneling ad clicks to DoorDash or Uber Eats costs 20% to 30% of gross revenue per order while surrendering guest contact ownership.
- Conversion contrast: Mobile-responsive e-commerce checkout flows convert visitors into orders at 3x to 5x higher rates than static image or PDF menus.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- **Replace "Boosted Posts" with Intent-Driven Search:** Reallocate social media boost budgets into optimizing Google Business Profile keywords and local citations where diners are actively searching with hungry intent.
- **Strict Influencer Accountability:** If partnering with creators, require: (1) proof of audience geography (minimum 70% residing within a 5-to-10 mile radius), (2) a unique trackable coupon code for direct ordering, and (3) usage rights to run their short-form video content as a paid ad.
- **Plug the Leaky Bucket First:** Never invest in paid advertising until the digital ordering website has high-resolution dish imagery, clear pricing, and 1-click mobile checkout above the fold.
