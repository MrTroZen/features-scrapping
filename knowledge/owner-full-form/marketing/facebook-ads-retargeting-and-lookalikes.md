# Key Information

- Breaks down three high-ROI Meta (Facebook/Instagram) ad strategies for independent restaurants, plus a direct catering acquisition framework:
  1. **High-Intent Cart/Visitor Retargeting:** Intercepts warm prospects who visited the online ordering site but failed to convert. Average restaurant website conversion rate is only 3%, leaving 97% unconverted due to temporary distraction or indecision.
  2. **Lookalike Audience Prospecting:** Ingests high-LTV customer lists from POS or online ordering databases to identify geographically proximate lookalike consumers who mirror the demographic and behavioral traits of top regulars.
  3. **Custom Audience Retention & Habit Reinforcement:** Serves low-cost reminder ads to past customer lists to combat natural dining habit drop-off and prompt re-orders.
  4. **Direct Catering Promotion:** Uses custom audience lists to solicit high-ticket corporate/party catering orders with a direct 15% discount, preempting third-party catering marketplaces (e.g. ezCater) that extract 30%+ commissions.

# Numbers / Calculations

- Restaurant website baseline: Only ~3% of first-time website visitors convert into immediate orders (97% bounce rate without retargeting).
- Retargeting ROI (Brooklyn Pizza case study): $500 ad spend generated 70+ orders and over $3,000 in direct sales in 30 days (> 6:1 ROAS).
- Meta CPM benchmark: Local restaurant ads average under $15.00 per 1,000 impressions. Re-engaging 200 top regulars 5 times per month costs ~$15.00 total in monthly ad spend.
- Retention campaign return: Consistent 5x to 10x ROAS ($5–$10 in sales per $1 spent) on custom audience past-customer ads (Otavio's generated 200 incremental orders and $8,000+ monthly revenue).
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC): Talkin' Tacos achieved sub-$10 CAC using lookalike audiences, driving 100+ new monthly customers.
- Direct catering margins: Offering a 15% direct discount yields significantly higher net profit than marketplace aggregators taking 30%+ in commission fees.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- **Precision Retargeting Parameters:** Build website custom audiences filtered strictly to users who spent > 60 seconds on site within the past 14 days, within city radius, excluding completed order thank-you pages.
- **Introductory Free-Delivery Hook:** For lookalike prospecting, use a limited-time "Free Delivery on Direct Orders" code paired with multi-photo carousel imagery of signature dishes rather than price-slashing food discounts.
- **Direct Catering Defense:** Deploy summer and holiday catering ad creatives directly to past corporate and group diners to keep high-margin catering prep in-house rather than ceding orders to ezCater.
