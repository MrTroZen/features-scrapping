# Key Information

- This major mistake that costs restaurant owners thousands of dollars is partnering with global influencers it can be so tempting to see that pizza page on Instagram that has a million followers and think to yourself if I could get posted to those million followers that would give me a huge amount of new customers but those million followers are distributed all around the world not in your town only a tiny fraction are actually near you and have the ability to become your customers and the vast majority of those people are globally distributed and you have to pay for an audience that might only have a tiny portion of people that are relevant to you.
