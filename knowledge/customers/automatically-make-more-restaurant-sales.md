# Key Information

- If you're a restaurant owner and you're not utilizing automated marketing you will lose business my friend Omar the owner of T and Tacos who wanted to boost his restaurant online sales so what he did is he created an automated campaign that sent a text after customers got their order it waited about an hour and then it said hey how was your experience leave us a review he just wanted to prompt the customer after they'd eaten that their thoughts would be more likely to be shared on Google reviews and that quickly helped him rack up more than 100 f-star reviews this in turn boosted their conversion rate and led to more search traffic and made him more popular on Google Driving more customer and negative reviews were also automatically flagged and forwarded to his manager so that customers could see that they listen and care about what they had to say.
