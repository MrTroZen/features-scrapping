# Key Information

- Any good loyalty program constantly reminds customers about the points they already have and reminds them to redeem those loyalty points before they expire because that drives urgency to act this might seem stupid but it's super powerful psychologically because it Taps into this psychological hack called completion bias which is basically the desire for humans to finish what they start if you watch Netflix I'm sure you've finished a show that probably kind of sucked towards the end but you decided to finish anyway right in loyalty programs it's the same deal not that the reward sucks but when customers feel that that they're that close to a reward and they've come this far they want to finish what they've started and get that prize.
