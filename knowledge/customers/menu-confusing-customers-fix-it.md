# Key Information

- If your restaurant's menu doesn't look like this, then pay attention because it might be costing your restaurant in sales.
- The first thing is consolidate categories.
- Your restaurant's categories need to be under 10, the data shows, because otherwise it becomes very overwhelming for guests.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- The third thing that your online menu absolutely needs is the right modifier order, which should be requirements, additions, subtractions, and extras because that matches the guest's psychology and makes it that much more likely that they'll view ordering from you as easy.
