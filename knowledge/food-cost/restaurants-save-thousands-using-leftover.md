# Key Information

- In Indian restaurants, the way butter chicken is made is they take yesterday's tandoori chicken and they add some fresh butter on top of it because that tandoori chicken is no longer fresh. So, they need a way to spruce it up.
- Grocery stores often chop up fruit that's about to expire and put it into a fruit salad, a lot of other restaurants put it into soups.
- Make use of the ingredients that are soon going to expire by creating a new dish altogether from them, reusing the ingredients that you've already got sunk cost.
