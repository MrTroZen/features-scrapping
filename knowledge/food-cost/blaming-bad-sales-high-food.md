# Key Information

- Food cost % is usage divided by sales.
- When sales dip and usage holds flat, the percentage spikes on its own.
- $3,000 in usage on $10,000 in sales lands at 30%.

# Numbers / Calculations

- $3,000 in usage on $10,000 in sales lands at 30%.
- Same $3,000 in usage on an $8,000 week lands at 37.5%.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- The usage side is still where control happens: accurate inventory counts, consistent portioning, and a tight handle on waste and theft.
