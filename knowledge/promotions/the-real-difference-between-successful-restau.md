# Key Information

- A lot of the upand cominging entrepreneurs don't see this until it's too late which is that Innovation doesn't come from being completely original and trying to into it what it is that the customer wants it comes from watching very closely at what that customer is buying from other brands that is a tremendous source of innovation because it tells you their preferences that they're expressing through how they spend money which is what you get from studying all of the other successful gourmet sandwich Concepts you saw sandwich taking off with Subway and Quiznos but at the same time you saw that these smaller independent players like Joe were crushing it at a very high price point and you wanted to merge the two Trends to create this brand that you thought would be explosive that's right or how Sam Walton built Walmart he famously was just constantly traveling to the United States and looking at all of the other retailers with the notepad figuring out what they did better than him and then testing that out at Walmart and when it worked he took it but it's so against this wisdom that success comes from originality when success actually comes from being a student of what works and then copying it and Innovation is blending multiple things that work to create a lot more value for the customer.
