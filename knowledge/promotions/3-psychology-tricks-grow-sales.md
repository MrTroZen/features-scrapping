# Key Information

- Want higher restaurant sales without discounting?
- Make choosing easier.
- Put every menu item on equal footing. Create a clear **Start Here** section with five dishes that are both popular *and* high-margin.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- Weekly menu-growth framework: Pick one profitable item to promote Make one placement, label, or checkout change Track sales mix, attachment rate, and margin by daypart Keep what wins; remove what doesn t.
