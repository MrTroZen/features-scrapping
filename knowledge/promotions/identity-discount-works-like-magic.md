# Key Information

- Does discounting work in increasing restaurant sales profitably? Heck yes, it would. I call it the identity discount. And this one is actually the most powerful for getting new customers. My friend Juliana gets the credit for this one. Her daughter's school had a bulletin board with a bunch of community announcements. So, she put up a poster telling people that SOS Waka was owned by another family at that school.
- They could get a special school discount from ordering from her. Within just 6 months, she got 75 new customers from this strategy. She has since set up partnerships with all of her community places, including the nearby church that they go.
- The other places that her community gathers. The reason this works.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- Is that the identity of people is the most powerful factor in how they make buying decisions.
