# Key Information

- Chipotle employees were bragging to each other about how they sold over 1,000 burritos per day.
- Most restaurants start by making their menu as big as possible.
- The thinking goes that if you have a big menu with a lot of options, each of those options will be able to appeal to a specific type of customer.

# Numbers / Calculations

- He knew that the typical fast food restaurant was only able to serve less than 50 orders per hour at the time and basically designed his entire concept around this assembly line approach that was able to double his throughput and instead based his menu around just four dishes, bowls, salads, burritos, and tacos.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- Those four dishes enabled him to have increased throughput and gave him the opportunity to master their execution.
