# Key Information

- There's another menu market fit mistake that kills restaurants. Using any language that doesn't reflect what people are actually typing into Google.
- , sometimes restaurant owners think that it will make their concept more authentic if they actually put the language of the cuisine type as the item name. An example of this would be if Jesus called his bean cheese burrito something like burrito confirles eo. It would be a problem because when Google would be searching for bean cheese burrito, they wouldn't be getting a direct match on that keyword. So, it wouldn't actually go to his menu.
