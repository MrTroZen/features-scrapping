# Key Information

- Your menu is losing you money and you don't even know it.
- Research shows that items placed within the golden triangle receive significantly more attention than those placed anywhere else on the menu.
- Triangle is center, top right, top left exactly where your customer's eyes land first every single time they open your menu.

# Numbers / Calculations

- Restaurants that engineer their menus around this pattern typically see a 10 to 15% increase in profits without changing a single recipe or spending a dollar on marketing.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- If they're not sitting in the center, top right, or top left move them before your next service.
