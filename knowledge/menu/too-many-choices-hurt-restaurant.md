# Key Information

- What's the ideal number of menu items for a restaurant? We've done a ton of research on this.
- We've found that the ideal number for maximizing profitability is six appetizers, which allows for just enough variety without overwhelming customers. Beverages, we've found the ideal number for is eight. That includes four signature cocktails or mocktails, which often have higher margins than a regular cocktail. For entre, the ideal number is going to be eight.
- A focused selection of entre allows for easier decision-making.
