# Key Information

- A slow economy makes most operators reach for the discount.
- Cheaper entrees, happy hour, anything to keep the check average from scaring people off.
- There's a better play.

# Numbers / Calculations

- People will skip the $30 dinner and still buy a $9 matcha.
- A $3 to $5 upgrade a guest can say yes to without thinking about it.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- The big spends get cut first.
