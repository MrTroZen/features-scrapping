# Key Information

- Let's price a cocktail together.
- Your Tom Collins sells for $8.50.
- You price it off based off of ingredients, gin, lemon, soda, and aimed for about 33% poor cost.

# Numbers / Calculations

- Your Tom Collins sells for $8.50.
- Now, if your target is 25% beverage cost, that Tom Collins should be closer to $10.
- That's roughly $120 you're left on the table.
- We're talking about $1,400 lost from that one cocktail.
