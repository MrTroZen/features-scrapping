# Key Information

- Why boosting Facebook posts actually just leads to lost money without an increase in sale the first major reason is called lack of intent what I mean by lack of intent is if you think about why people are on Facebook it's not to discover new restaurants it's to connect with friends and family or entertain themselves whereas when you're boosting a post about chicken parm that you just added to your menu that doesn't feel relevant to the people that are there to connect with friends and family so you have to interrupt what they're there to do and they don't have an intent to buy what you offering.
