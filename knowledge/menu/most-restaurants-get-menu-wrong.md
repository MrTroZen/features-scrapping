# Key Information

- Here's the hidden reason why.
- Many restaurants fail. They lack menu market fit. The dishes on their menu don't actually represent what people nearby are craving. You used to have to guess.
- Dishes would resonate, but now you can actually see the data on your exact area.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- How many people are searching for that specific dish. Here's how. First, use Google keyword planner to research what people actually search for in your area. You look for the dishes that have over 250 monthly searches. Step two is adding those dishes to your online menu. And the third step here is keeping your menu focused.
