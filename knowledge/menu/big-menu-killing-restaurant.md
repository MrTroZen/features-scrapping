# Key Information

- Adding more dishes doesn't automatically create more demand.
- A five-page menu can actually create choice overload, slower ordering, more inventory, more waste, longer ticket times, and a harder operation to train.
- Focus your menu: Identify the dishes customers actually want and your kitchen executes best.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- You need more customers ordering the food you're already great at making.
