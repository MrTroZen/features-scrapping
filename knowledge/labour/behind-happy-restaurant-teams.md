# Key Information

- The most successful restaurants aren't using technology to fully replace workers. They're using it to turn their workers into more like business partners. Mario Delpero, the founder.
- Former CEO of Menoscino Farms, actually taught me this. He taught me that instead of just telling employees what to do, he shares at the restaurant level their unit metrics with each employee. His managers know his food cost percentages on any given day, his labor ratios, and the profit margins on different menu items. When employees understand that selling a high margin appetizer helps everyone keep their jobs.
- Have a higher average wage, they naturally become better salespeople.
