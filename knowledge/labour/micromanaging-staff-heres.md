# Key Information

- Micromanaging your team doesn't raise your standards.
- A 2025 study found that micromanagement correlates strongly with lower employee productivity, lower trust in leadership, and reduced innovation.
- When you check on everything and approve every decision, your team stops thinking independently.

# Numbers / Calculations

- Restaurant turnover is already nearly 80% industry-wide and it costs up to $5,864 to hire and train a single new staff member.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- They build systems that manage the standard and then they hire people who take pride in hitting it.
