# Key Information

- The number one reason restaurants fail has nothing to do with the food.
- Most owners pour everything into perfecting their menu and rightfully.
- The restaurants that close aren't closing because the food was bad.

# Numbers / Calculations

- A $500K startup needs to bring in $600K to $750K a year before you even start talking about profit.
- You also need 20% of your revenue sitting in savings at all times.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- Customer retention, email marketing, keeping the customers you already have coming back that's where your money goes first.
