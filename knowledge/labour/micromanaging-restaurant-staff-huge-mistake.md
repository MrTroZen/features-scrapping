# Key Information

- Never micromanage your staff.
- When I see someone over-cheesing a pizza, my first instinct may be to jump and become the portion control police.
- The moment I start hovering, I'm training the team to only care when I am standing there.

# Useful Method / Takeaway

- We train them to forecast weekly sales, plan orders accordingly, and to track COGS daily.
