# Key Information

- Want to boost team morale without spending a dime? Here's the fastest way I know. Praise publicly, but specific. Not, "Hey, great job team Taylor. You jumped on dish without anyone asking. Thank you, Jamal. You caught that wrong ticket before it hit the table. That saved us a customer complaint." Thank you, Maria. You upsoed three desserts. That's the kind of stuff that adds up. Thank you, Louise. You stayed an hour past your schedule time to help the team get through the rush. Thank you.
- Much. Specific praise hits harder. It tells people what exactly they're doing, right? So, they keep doing it.
