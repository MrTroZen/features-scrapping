# Key Information

- Most people blame the distributor or "inflation." But the real answer starts on a farm.
- It takes 10 gallons of milk to make just 1 lb of cheese When temps rise, cows get heat stressed and produce less milk Less milk = tighter supply = cheddar block prices spike.
- No one in the supply chain is gouging you.
